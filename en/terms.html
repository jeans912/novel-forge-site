Version 2.40 · Effective 2026-11-05

1. License

Your purchase grants a license to install and use NovelForge on one device per license key. Moving to another device requires deactivating the old one or contacting support. The trial period is 14 days with full functionality.

2. Your data

  • Your manuscripts are stored on your own computer. NovelForge does not upload them automatically.
  • On first run the app creates a local data directory for settings, logs, and backup state.
  • AI call logs are stored locally only and can be viewed, exported, or cleared.
  • When you use cloud AI features, the content of that request is sent to the provider you selected. NovelForge also offers redaction and a “never use cloud” setting.

  • Credit packs are non-refundable once redeemed; unused credits do not expire.
  • Licensed users may supply their own API keys and pay providers directly.
