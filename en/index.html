FAQ

Questions people ask

Is my manuscript uploaded anywhere?

No. Files stay on your machine. Content is only sent when you explicitly run an AI action, and you can set AI permissions to “never use cloud”, or enable redaction (names replaced with tokens).

Do I need an NVIDIA GPU?

No. Writing, auditing and export all work without one. The visual novel has a template mode that generates art locally without any model. Local AI art requires ComfyUI; cloud AI just needs an API key.

Do AI images cost extra?

Yes: 1 credit per image. Packs are $4/150 and $6/300. You can also bring your own API key and pay the provider directly. Template mode is free.

Can I use it on a second computer?

One license covers one machine while activated. Contact us to transfer your license.

Why does Windows warn about an unknown publisher?

The installer is not code-signed yet. Click “More info → Run anyway”. We are working on signing.
