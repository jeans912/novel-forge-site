FAQ

Questions you may have

Do I have to pay for AI usage?

No. The app charges nothing for AI: plug in your own API key and pay your provider directly. Local ComfyUI is free.

Can I sell the games I make?

Yes. The Galgame edition includes commercial export rights; build a Windows exe with Ren'Py. Check your voice/asset licenses before selling.

Can I export visual novels in the free version?

Yes, without a watermark. The paid edition only adds multi-route Galgame tools and commercial export.

Why "Unknown publisher" on Windows?

The installer is not code-signed yet. Click "More info → Run anyway".

Does it work offline?

Writing, backups, exports and local ComfyUI work offline. AI features need your key/provider.
